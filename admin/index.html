Campaign Overlays

Create reusable campaign skins for events, launches and seasonal promotions. Upload the finished artwork, schedule it, preview it and turn it on without another deployment.

Artwork specifications: Desktop hero 2400 × 1350 px (16:9). Mobile hero 1080 × 1920 px (9:16). Partner logo PNG or WebP with transparent background, ideally 1200 px wide. Use RGB/sRGB. JPG, PNG or WebP, maximum 12 MB each. Keep important text at least 120 px from desktop edges and 90 px from mobile edges. The website adds CTA buttons below the artwork, so do not bake buttons into the image.
