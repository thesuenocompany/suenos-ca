# Contest Admin simplification — 2026-08-08

Campaign-first contest administration.

- Retail master owns campaign-wide setup and shared creative.
- Retailer pages appear as compact operational rows beneath the master.
- Retail rows show only public state, dates, entries, page access, poster and social downloads.
- Four social creatives are visible as thumbnails on every retailer row and can be downloaded individually.
- Social ZIP can be generated directly from the retailer row.
- Secondary Duplicate / Archive actions are moved under More.
- Retailer Manage view hides master-oriented setup steps and focuses on Retailer + Dates.
- Opening an editor hides the campaign list so Admin presents one job at a time.
