Sueños Tequila FAQ

Clear answers about the brand, its founder, ownership, origin, current product and where to find it.

Quick answer: Sueños Tequila is a Canadian-owned tequila brand founded by Gord Erickson in British Columbia. Sueños Blanco is made in Jalisco, México, from 100% Blue Weber agave and is bottled at 40% ABV in a 750 mL bottle.

What is Sueños Tequila?

Sueños Tequila is a Canadian-owned tequila brand built around approachable quality, shared experiences and the idea that “Paradise Is a State of Sueños.”

Who founded Sueños Tequila?

Canadian entrepreneur Gord Erickson founded Sueños Tequila. The brand began with his connection to Mexico and the idea of bringing a sense of escape home.

Is Sueños Tequila Canadian-owned?

Yes. Sueños is Canadian-owned and rooted in British Columbia.

Where is Sueños Tequila made?

Sueños Blanco is made in Jalisco, México, the production origin stated throughout the current Sueños website.

Is Sueños made with 100% agave?

Yes. Sueños Blanco is made with 100% Blue Weber agave.

What Sueños product is currently featured?

The current product is Sueños Blanco, a blanco tequila bottled at 40% ABV in a 750 mL bottle.

Which cocktails can I make with Sueños Blanco?

The recipe collection includes the Sueños Margarita, Paloma del Sueño, Ranch Water, Mexican Mule, Cantarito, El Diablo, Matador, Tequila Sunrise and Tequila Old Fashioned. Browse all cocktail recipes.
