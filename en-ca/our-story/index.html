OUR STORY

Paradise Is A State of Sueños.

A Canadian-owned tequila brand founded by Gord Erickson and made in Jalisco.

Founded by
Gord Erickson
Founded by Gord EricksonCanadian-ownedRooted in British ColumbiaMade in Jalisco

Most days ask a lot. Sueños was created to ask less.

Founded by Canadian entrepreneur Gord Erickson, Sueños grew from his connection to Mexico and the belief that paradise can be a feeling you bring home.

Rooted in British Columbia and made in Jalisco, Sueños is for shared tables, music turned up, lingering sunlight and ordinary nights worth remembering.

Every bottle carries that journey from Canada to Mexico and back again.

Canadian-owned. Made in Jalisco.
