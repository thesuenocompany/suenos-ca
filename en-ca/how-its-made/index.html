HOW IT’S MADE

How Sueños Blanco Is Made

Six stages take Sueños Blanco from Blue Weber agave in Jalisco to a clean, bright tequila.

Follow the journey ↓
100%Blue Weber Agave

From Agave to Bottle

The process combines agricultural work, careful timing and skilled choices that begin years before the bottle reaches the shelf.

01Agave 02Harvest 03Cook 04Extract 05Ferment and Distill 06Bottle
01
01 Agave

Blue Weber agave grows in Jalisco.

Tequila begins with one plant. Blue Weber agave spends years in Jalisco building the natural sugars that make the entire process possible.

Long before distillation, the future flavour is already taking shape in the soil, sun and plant.

02
02 Harvest

Mature agave is harvested by skilled jimadores.

When the agave is mature, jimadores cut away its long leaves to reveal the piña, the dense heart that holds the sugars destined to become tequila.

It is skilled, physical work and a reminder that tequila is agricultural before it is anything else.

03
03 Cook

The agave piñas are cooked.

The harvested piñas are cooked so their complex carbohydrates break down into fermentable sugars.

This is the turning point where a field crop begins its transformation into spirit.

04
04 Extract

The cooked agave is crushed and extracted.

After cooking, the agave is processed to collect its sweet liquid. That juice carries the plant’s character into fermentation.

It is a quieter stage, but it decides how cleanly the flavour moves forward.

05
05 Ferment and Distill

Agave sugars become tequila.

Yeast converts the agave sugars into alcohol during fermentation, building aroma and character along the way.

Distillation then refines the liquid, creating clarity, lift and the clean profile expected from Sueños Blanco.

06
06 Bottle

Sueños Blanco is bottled at 40% ABV.

Sueños Blanco is bottled to keep fresh agave, citrus lift, natural pepper and a clean finish at the centre of the experience.

Bottling preserves the clean, bright profile of Sueños Blanco for sipping or mixing in cocktails.

THE RESULT

Bright agave, citrus, mineral notes and a clean finish.

The character of Sueños Blanco begins in the agave fields and carries through every stage of production.
