Tequila Cocktail

The Don Terry

Tequila, pineapple and soda. That’s the whole idea.

A light, tropical highball made with Sueños Blanco, pineapple juice and chilled soda water. Simple enough for any afternoon and bright enough to earn a name of its own.

Highball glassEasy3 minutesPineapple wedge

Ingredients

  • 45 mL (1½ oz) Sueños Blanco Tequila
  • 60 mL (2 oz) pineapple juice
  • 90 mL (3 oz) chilled soda water
  • Ice
  • Pineapple wedge for garnish

How to Make It

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add Sueños Blanco and pineapple juice.
  3. Top with chilled soda water and stir gently.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and serve.
  5. Serve confidently. Confidence accounts for roughly 40% of bartending.
Glassware

Highball glass

Difficulty

Easy

Time

3 minutes

Garnish

Pineapple wedge

Serves

1 cocktail
