Tequila Cocktail

Matador

Tropical, smooth and evening-friendly.

A sleek tequila classic combining pineapple and lime for a tropical serve that still stays clean and balanced.

Coupe glassEasy3 minutesPineapple wedge and lime

Ingredients

  • 60 mL (2 oz) Sueños Blanco Tequila
  • 45 mL (1½ oz) chilled pineapple juice
  • 15 mL (½ oz) fresh lime juice
  • 7 mL (¼ oz) agave syrup, optional
  • Ice
  • Pineapple wedge and lime for garnish

How to Make It

  1. Chill a coupe glass.
  2. Add Sueños Blanco, pineapple juice, lime juice and agave syrup if using to a shaker with ice.
  3. Shake until cold and strain into the chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and lime and serve.
Glassware

Coupe glass

Difficulty

Easy

Time

Garnish
