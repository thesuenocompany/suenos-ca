What does 100% agave mean?

On a tequila label, “100% agave” means all the sugars used to make it came from Blue Weber agave. It doesn’t tell you how long it aged, how it tastes, or whether anything was added after distillation.

Sueños Tequila

Two categories, one agave

Both categories start with Blue Weber agave. The Tequila Regulatory Council (CRT) explains the difference: “Tequila 100% de agave” uses only agave sugars and is bottled at origin. The category labelled simply “Tequila” must get at least 51% of its fermentable sugars from Blue Weber agave and can use up to 49% from other permitted sugar sources.

Is 100% agave the same as additive-free?

No. It describes where the fermentable sugars came from, not everything that happened after distillation. The Mexican tequila standard treats sugar origin and permitted finishing separately. Don’t use one claim as a shortcut for the other.

What does the NOM number mean?

The number alongside “NOM” identifies the authorised producer, as shown in the CRT’s labelling guidance. It helps you trace who made the tequila. It isn’t a rating, and separate brands can share it.
