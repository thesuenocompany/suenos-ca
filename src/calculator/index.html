    Supplier assumptions

    AASJ October 20 quote: MOQ 5,000 L, 55% ABV, USD 4.65 “all-in” to BC DAP, but price unit not stated. Per-litre basis is provisional. Canadian customs clearance excluded. Sergio March 25 liquid-only: MXN 98 / 92 / 85 per L at 1,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 L and 50% ABV. July 10 CAD 12.68/L estimate is historical, excludes taxes and contains tote-line inconsistencies. No agave ratio is verified. The private source messages were reviewed for this model; they are not uploaded publicly.

    Model conventions

    All outputs CAD. Planning production and packaging costs and margins are illustrative inputs, not received quotes. Input GST is treated as recoverable. Loss is applied after blending; loss-duty relief is not guaranteed, so add any assessed duty liability manually; theoretical blend volume is charged for ingredients and co-packing. Full unit counts are rounded down. Comparison scenarios are alternatives; never sum them. Keg and can duties are identical per finished litre at 5% and 7%. Figures are rounded only for display.
